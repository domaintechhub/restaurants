## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an elevated editorial food and beverage discovery platform, capturing the cosmopolitan spirit, warmth, and culinary vibrancy of urban dining destinations. Drawing inspiration from high-end gastronomic journals combined with modern booking ergonomics, the aesthetic marries sensory culinary storytelling with seamless digital utility.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Warm & Convivial:** Evoking aromatic kitchens, terracotta tiles, spice bazaars, and sun-drenched terrace lunches.
- **Curated & Authoritative:** Instilling trust through editorial clarity, discerning food criticism, and structured culinary guides.
- **Effortless & Tactile:** Providing responsive, frictionless booking and filtering flows reminiscent of world-class hospitality tools.

### Design Movement
- **Warm Editorial Modernism:** A hybrid aesthetic combining editorial serif typography, sun-warmed organic accents, pill-form interactive chips, and creamy ivory backgrounds that eliminate sterile cold whites in favor of comfortable reading surfaces.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from roasted spices, baked earthenware, and evening golden hours. 

- **Primary (`#E05A38` - Terracotta):** Anchors primary call-to-actions, booking reservation buttons, and active interactive indicators.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Deep Saffron Amber):** Highlights ratings, curated badge markers, and featured highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309` - Rich Spiced Cinnamon):** Accents culinary tags, category headers, and editorial subtext.
- **Neutral (`#1C1917` - Deep Espresso Charcoal):** Delivers contrast against warm surfaces, ensuring maximum legibility across editorial titles and UI elements.

### Surface Hierarchy
Surfaces are tiered on a warm continuum: the default canvas sits on `background-ivory` (`#FDFBF7`), while interactive and media cards rest on `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`) with borders framed in `border-subtle` (`#EDE5D8`) to maintain visual comfort without stark digital grayness.

## Typography

The typographical strategy pairs a transitional high-contrast serif (**Playfair Display**) for headlines with a clean, geometric humanist sans-serif (**Plus Jakarta Sans**) for operational UI, metadata, and body reading.

- **Editorial Headings:** Use `Playfair Display` for collection titles, neighborhood guides, and venue names to evoke a culinary magazine aesthetic.
- **Interface & Operational Text:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers rapid scanning speed across multi-attribute restaurant cards, filter chips, time slots, and pricing details.
- **Micro-labels & Badges:** `label-caps` provides uppercase legibility for tags like `ROOFTOP`, `FINE DINING`, or `PRICE TIER`.
